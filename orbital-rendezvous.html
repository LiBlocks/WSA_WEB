          How rendezvous works

          Close is not the same as matched

          KGOSS moves at about 2,100 m/s. You can fly right past it while only a few hundred metres away if your velocity is different. To dock, you have to be in the same place and moving the same way: your relative velocity must be close to zero.

          The lessons

          1. Read the map. Kerbin is in the middle. KGOSS (amber) circles at 200 km. Your craft is blue. Both orbit anticlockwise, and lower orbits go round faster.
          2. Prograde and retrograde. Prograde is the way you're moving; retrograde is the opposite.
          3. Burn. A prograde burn raises the far side of your orbit; a retrograde burn lowers it.
          4. Watch the orbit change. Ap and Pe mark your orbit's highest and lowest points.
          5. Plan a transfer. Burn when KGOSS is the right distance ahead so you reach its orbit just as it arrives. The closest-approach marker shows how near you'll pass.
          6. Match its motion. Near the closest approach, point against your relative velocity and burn until it's nearly zero.
          7. Reduce relative velocity. Close in slowly. Faster than the safe speed for your distance is unsafe.
          8. Final approach. The docking port faces backwards along KGOSS's path. Fly up the corridor behind it, nose on the docking axis, and touch the port gently.

          Controls

          Turn
          A D turn left and right. With Flight Assist on, the craft stops turning when you let go; with it off, it keeps spinning until you counter it.
          Main engine
          Hold Space to burn at the set throttle. W S throttle up and down, Z full, X minimum.
          Translate
          I K forward and back, J L left and right (or the arrow keys): small thrusters for docking.
          Fine control
          Hold Shift to turn and translate at a quarter of the rate.
          Flight Assist
          T on or off. Holds: 1 prograde, 2 retrograde, 3 toward KGOSS, 4 match velocity (against your relative velocity), 5 docking axis, 0 release.
          Time and view
          , . slower and faster time warp. V map or close-up, F map focus, C recentre, + − or the mouse wheel to zoom. P or Esc pause, H this help.
          Touch
          ⟲ ⟳ turn, hold BURN for the main engine, THR − + throttle. The four arrows translate. PRO, RETRO, TGT, MATCH and DOCK are the Flight Assist holds. « » change time warp.
          Tilt
          Optional: switch on Tilt to turn by tipping the device left and right. The game asks the browser for motion data only then, and only to steer. Calibrate sets how you're holding it as level. The buttons always work too.

          About the simulation

          A simplified, flat (2D) model: Kerbin is a point mass with KSP's radius and gravity, and the craft and KGOSS feel only its pull (two-body gravity, no drag above 70 km, no out-of-plane motion). Both are stepped at 30 Hz at every time warp. Relative velocity is measured in KGOSS's own turning frame, so zero means you keep your place beside it. Delta-v is how much more velocity change your propellant can give.
