What WSA Does

Reusable Launch Vehicles

The Aegis family launches WSA's missions while its booster recovery is tested and improved. Lyra is the larger next-generation family in development.

Autonomous Landing

AutoLand is WSA's own kOS software for landing boosters, written instead of relying on MechJeb. Version 2.3 has landed an Aegis booster on its target pad.

Orbital Infrastructure

KGOSS-1, assembled in Kerbin orbit module by module, is WSA's station and assembly hub.

Planetary Exploration

FAMMOK landers made WSA's first landings on the Mun, Minmus and Duna, and crewed missions have reached Duna and an asteroid.

Aircraft & Spaceplanes

Swift-1X, SSTO development, and atmospheric programs test flight-control concepts and reusable transportation.

Incremental Engineering

WSA learns from each test, documents failures honestly, and improves designs through rigorous iteration.

Recent Milestones

Demonstrated

AutoLand V2.3 Pad Landing

An Aegis booster touched down directly on its designated landing pad: AutoLand's first successful target-pad landing.

Completed

Minmus Firework

A crewed flight on an Aegis Mk1.3 landed on Minmus, planted a flag and splashed down back on Kerbin.

Flown

Astra-1 and LunaLink-1

A space telescope in a 561 km orbit round Kerbin, and an ion-engined relay satellite in orbit round the Mun.

Flown

Odysseus at Duna

WSA's fourth Duna attempt entered orbit around the planet, chose not to land with too little fuel, and returned to Kerbin.
