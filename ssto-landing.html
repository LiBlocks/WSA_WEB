Land the spaceplane

Your spaceplane is on approach to runway 09 at the WSA spaceport. Line up with the centreline, follow the glide path down, flare, touch down gently on the main wheels and brake to a stop on the runway.

Scenario

How to land

  1. Turn toward the runway and put the centreline under the nose.
  2. Lower the landing gear.
  3. Descend along the glide path: the PAPI lights left of the runway show two white and two red when you're on it.
  4. Keep about 65–75 m/s with the throttle.
  5. Over the runway, throttle to idle and raise the nose gently to flare.
  6. Touch down on the main wheels, wings level, then brake to a stop.

Controls

Pitch
W nose down · S nose up (like a control stick; swap below)
Roll · rudder
A D roll left and right · Q E rudder. On the runway they steer.
Throttle
R up · F down · X idle · Z full
Also
G gear · hold B to brake · T Flight Assist · V camera · M mouse steering · Space or Esc pause. Arrow keys work like W A S D.
Mouse
Choose Mouse above the view, then move the pointer from the centre toward where the nose should go; the wheel sets the throttle.
Touch
Stick: up lowers the nose, left and right roll. THR slider: throttle. GEAR, hold BRAKE, ◀ ▶ rudder, CAM: camera.
Tilt
Choose Tilt above the view to fly by tilting the device: tip it away to lower the nose, left or right to roll. Calibrate sets how you hold it as level.
