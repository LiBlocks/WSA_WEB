Dock with the station

Your spacecraft starts about 180 m from the station, slightly off the approach axis and drifting. Fly to the active docking port (green lights, marked PORT on the HUD) and dock gently.

Procedure

  1. Find the port reticle; read range and closing speed.
  2. Cancel unwanted drift.
  3. Line up: put the centre marker on the axis crosshairs and level the roll.
  4. Translate until the port reticle sits on the crosshairs.
  5. Close slowly, staying inside the corridor gates.
  6. Keep the prograde marker on the port and slow down as you get near.
  7. Touch at 0.2 m/s or less, within 0.25 m and 5°, rotating under 0.5°/s.

Controls

Touch
MOVE stick or arrows: translate · ▲ fwd / ▼ back: close in or back off · TURN stick or arrows: pitch and yaw · ↺ ↻: roll · FINE: gentler thrust. Choose Joystick or Buttons above the view.
Translate
I up · K down · J left · L right · H forward · N back
Rotate
W pitch up · S pitch down · A yaw left · D yaw right · Q roll left · E roll right
Also
Hold Shift for fine thrust · F Flight Assist · P or Esc pause

HUD symbols

  • Centre marker: where your docking port points
  • Port reticle: the active docking port
  • Prograde: where you are actually moving
  • Axis crosshairs: the ideal docking axis

Flight Assist is on: it gently damps rotation and sideways drift, but you still steer, line up and control the closing speed. This is a simplified local relative-motion docking simulator, not a full orbital rendezvous simulation or a validated spacecraft model.
