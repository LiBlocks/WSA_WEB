        Controls and help

        Camera
        Drag in the 3D view to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan. C or Reset view puts the camera back; F or Follow keeps it on the vehicle.
        Run
        Space start, pause and resume. R reset. , . slower and faster simulation. Replay re-runs your last experiment with exactly the same settings and inputs.
        Actions
        Each experiment's buttons show their key: K shut down or cut engines, I ignite, S or Enter stage, B burn, X cancel a planned burn, M map view, G guidance on and off, E end the experiment.
        Flying the booster
        With Who flies set to you: W S (or ↑ ↓) throttle, A D (or ← →) lean left and right, Q E lean forward and back. On a touch screen, hold the buttons in the corner of the view.
        Basic and Pro
        Basic shows the main settings in plain words. Pro shows every parameter with its symbol and unit, plus extra telemetry. Both use the same experiment: switching never resets it.
        Live settings
        Settings marked live change the experiment while it runs. Others apply when you reset.
