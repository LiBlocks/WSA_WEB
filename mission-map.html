Mission Map

Explore the Kerbol system, with the stock planets and the Outer Planets Mod. Pick a planet, moon or region to see the WSA missions documented there, then replay a mission step by step.

Approximate map. Sizes and distances are not to scale, and the bodies are drawn in an illustrative layout, not where they are now. Routes are schematic curves between recorded milestones, and replays show the order of events, not real elapsed time.

    Reading the map

    • Schematic route: a curve between recorded places, not a measured trajectory
    • Documented route (none recorded yet)
    • Planned or intended route that was not flown
    • Recorded event; select it to jump there
    • Mission position during the replay

    Bodies are not to scale and are shown in an illustrative layout. Moons appear as you zoom in to their planet. OPM marks bodies added or moved by the Outer Planets Mod.

    Drag to rotate. Right-drag, Shift-drag or two fingers to pan. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
