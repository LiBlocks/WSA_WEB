About WSA

WSA is a small, independent aerospace organization focused on developing reliable, reusable launch vehicles and autonomous flight systems. Founded in 2025, WSA pursues incremental engineering excellence through iterative vehicle development, rigorous testing, and continuous improvement. Our mission is to make reliable spaceflight accessible and demonstrate that autonomous systems can safely manage complex aerospace operations.

Strategic Goals

Reusable Launch Vehicles

Develop booster families designed for full recovery and rapid reuse, reducing the cost and environmental impact of spaceflight.

Autonomous Landing Technology

Advance automated guidance and control systems capable of precision landings in varied conditions without human intervention.

Orbital Infrastructure

Establish modular orbital platforms and docking capabilities to support sustained space operations and collaborative missions.

Crewed Spaceflight

Develop reliable systems for human spaceflight, prioritizing safety through extensive testing and redundancy.

Deep-Space Exploration

Extend WSA capabilities to beyond-Earth-orbit missions, developing advanced landing and precision navigation systems.

Incremental Engineering

Pursue continuous improvement through methodical development cycles, learning from each test and flight iteration.

Aegis booster successfully recovered after first full-scale powered flight test.

Work starts on Lyra family—next-generation reusable launch vehicle for higher-capacity missions.
Vehicle trades conducted. Three-variant architecture established (Selene, Sol, Astra). Initial systems specifications drafted.

Deep-space exploration and precision landing systems development commences.
Advanced guidance algorithms developed. Testing with subscale models ongoing. Landing site analysis for multi-body missions completed.
