Goals & Achievements

Long-term objectives and verified accomplishments that define WSA's progress toward sustainable spaceflight.

Strategic Objectives

These long-term goals guide WSA's engineering work:

Reliable Booster Recovery

Develop reusable boosters that routinely survive descent, land on target, and return to service. Not just surviving—consistently landing safely and protecting vehicle integrity for reflight.

Autonomous Precision Landing

Build guidance systems capable of landing spacecraft at designated locations with minimal landing error, gentle touchdown speeds, and no pilot intervention required.

Sustained Orbital Operations

Establish persistent orbital infrastructure and capability for regular crew rotation, logistics, docking, fuel transfer, and on-orbit assembly without ground support for every maneuver.

Planetary Exploration

Extend landing capability to the Mun, Minmus, and Duna. Develop systems for surface operations, return capability, and increasingly ambitious mission profiles.

Crewed Spaceflight

Reliably transport crew to orbit and beyond. Provide safe return, rescue capability, and redundant systems for human missions. Make crewed spaceflight routine and safe.

Incremental Technology Development

Build WSA's own guidance systems, control algorithms, and mission-planning tools. Understand the principles behind reliable spaceflight rather than outsourcing complex decisions.
