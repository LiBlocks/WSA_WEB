Mission Records

Every WSA mission on record, from the same records as the Mission Map. Each event says where it comes from: WSA's earlier records, the operator's own history, or WSA's KSP save files. Unknown details are left out rather than guessed, and open questions are listed with the mission.

Reading the Records

FAMMOK numbering

FAMMOK is WSA's lander and upper-stage family. Mission numbers mark steps in capability and ambition, from the first Mun landing (FAMMOK-1) to the first Minmus and Duna landings (FAMMOK-2) and a Duna rescue mission (FAMMOK-3).

Statuses

Missions are not simply successes or failures. A mission can be completed, flown with problems, partially successful, stranded (intact but unable to get home), lost, or still only planned. A deliberate decision, such as Odysseus turning back from Duna, is recorded as a decision.

Sources

Facts from the save files are read without changing them: vessel states, flags and docking records. A save file's name alone never counts as evidence that something happened.
