        Controls and help

        The goal
        Land the booster upright on the pad: slow the descent with the engines, lean the booster to move sideways, and stand it up straight for the last few metres. The legs swing down by themselves at 150 m.
        Attitude
        W S (or ↑ ↓) pitch the booster forward and back; A D (or ← →) roll it left and right. The engine pushes along the booster, so leaning it moves you that way.
        Throttle
        Hold Shift to throttle up and Ctrl to throttle down; Z full throttle, X engines off. (Ctrl together with W is a browser shortcut on some systems: use X or let go of W first.)
        Attitude hold
        Space switches it on and off. On: the booster holds the lean you ask for and stands itself upright when you let go. Off: the stick fires the thrusters directly, and the booster keeps turning until you stop it.
        Camera
        C cycles Follow, Side view, Pad view and Free camera (or press 1–4). In Free camera, drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Reset returns to the default view.
        Guidance
        The top view shows the pad, your velocity and (on Easy and Normal) where you would hit the ground if you stopped burning; the orange ring shows the same point in 3D. The attitude dial shows your lean and the limit for touchdown. On Easy, a cue tells you when to start the landing burn.
        Touch screens
        Drag the stick on the left to lean; use the throttle slider and + / − on the right. Tilt is optional: switch it on to lean by tilting the device (the browser may ask for permission), and press Level to set how you are holding it. The buttons always work too.
        Other keys
        P or Esc pause, R restart, H this help.
